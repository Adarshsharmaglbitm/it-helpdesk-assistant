# New Joiner IT Onboarding

## Before day one
The hiring manager raises an onboarding request in the self-service portal at least 5 business days before the start date. This triggers creation of the employee's company account, email mailbox, and laptop preparation. Late requests may mean the laptop is not ready on day one.

## Day one
- Collect your laptop from the IT desk on your first morning. Bring a government-issued photo ID.
- IT gives you a temporary password, which you must change at first sign-in.
- Enroll in multi-factor authentication within your first 3 days.
- Complete the mandatory security awareness training in the learning portal within your first week.

## Default access
Every new employee automatically gets email, Teams, the intranet, the HR portal, and the learning portal. Access to team-specific systems (for example, finance tools, code repositories, or customer databases) is **not** given by default.

## Requesting additional access
Team-specific access must be requested through the self-service portal under **Access > Application Access**. The request goes to your manager and then to the application owner for approval. Most access is granted within 1 business day after both approvals.

## Contractors
Contractor accounts are created with an end date that matches the contract. Accounts are disabled automatically on the end date unless the manager requests an extension at least 5 business days in advance.
