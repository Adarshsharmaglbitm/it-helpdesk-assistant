# Password Reset and Password Policy

## Self-service password reset
You can reset your own password at any time without contacting IT:
1. Go to the self-service password portal at `passwordreset.company.internal`.
2. Enter your company email address.
3. Verify your identity with the MFA app or the backup code sent to your registered personal mobile number.
4. Choose a new password that meets the policy below.

The new password syncs to email, VPN, and Wi-Fi within about 15 minutes. Laptops that are off the office network pick up the new password the next time they connect to the VPN.

## Password policy
- Minimum length is 14 characters.
- Use a mix of at least three of: uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols.
- You cannot reuse any of your last 5 passwords.
- Passwords expire every 90 days. You will get email reminders 14 days and 3 days before expiry.
- Never share your password with anyone, including IT staff. IT will never ask for your password.

## Account lockout
Your account locks automatically for 30 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts. You can either wait 30 minutes or unlock it immediately using the self-service portal ("Unlock my account").

A common cause of repeated lockouts is an old password saved on your phone's email app or on a second device. Update the saved password on every device after a reset.

## If you cannot reset it yourself
If you have lost access to your MFA method and backup number, call the IT Service Desk. The agent will verify your identity using your employee ID and manager confirmation before issuing a temporary password.
