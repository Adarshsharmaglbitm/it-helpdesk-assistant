# Slow Laptop Performance

## Quick fixes to try first
1. **Restart the laptop.** Many issues come from the laptop running for days without a restart. Use Restart, not Shut Down, so pending updates install properly.
2. **Close unused apps and browser tabs.** Browsers with dozens of open tabs can use several gigabytes of memory.
3. **Check free disk space.** Keep at least 20 GB free. Empty the Recycle Bin and clear the Downloads folder.
4. **Install pending updates** from the Software Center (Windows) or Self Service app (macOS).

## Find what is using resources
- **Windows**: Press Ctrl + Shift + Esc to open Task Manager and sort the Processes tab by CPU or Memory.
- **macOS**: Open Activity Monitor from Applications > Utilities and sort by % CPU or Memory.

If one app is using most of the CPU or memory, close and reopen it. Video calls, large spreadsheets, and code builds are normal heavy users.

## Things that are expected to use resources
Security scans run every Wednesday at 12:00 noon and may slow the laptop for about 30 minutes. Do not try to stop or uninstall the security agent; doing so triggers an alert and your laptop may be isolated from the network.

## When to raise a ticket
Raise a ticket under **Hardware > Performance** if the laptop is still slow after a restart and updates, if it overheats or the fan runs loudly all the time, or if it is more than 4 years old. Laptops older than 4 years are eligible for replacement under the hardware refresh program.
