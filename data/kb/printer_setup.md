# Printing and Printer Setup

## How office printing works
All office printers use secure print release. Jobs you send go into a central print queue and are only printed when you tap your employee badge on any office printer. This means you can print from your desk and collect the pages at whichever printer is closest. Jobs that are not released are deleted after 24 hours.

## Adding the office printer to your laptop
- **Windows**: Open **Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers & scanners > Add device**, then select `SecurePrint-Queue`. The driver installs automatically.
- **macOS**: Open **System Settings > Printers & Scanners > Add Printer**, choose `SecurePrint-Queue`, and select "Generic PostScript Printer" as the driver.

You must be connected to the office network or the VPN to add the queue.

## Registering your badge
The first time you tap your badge on a printer, it asks you to sign in with your company email and password. After that, your badge is linked and future taps release your jobs instantly.

## Troubleshooting
- **Job does not appear at the printer**: Make sure you selected `SecurePrint-Queue` and not a "Microsoft Print to PDF" option. Check that you are on the office network or VPN.
- **Badge not recognized**: Re-register by signing in at the printer. Badges replaced in the last 24 hours may take a day to sync.
- **Paper jam or toner empty**: Use the printer's on-screen instructions. For empty toner, contact Facilities, not IT.

## Printing at home
Company laptops are not configured for home printers by default. Printing confidential documents at home is not allowed. For non-confidential documents, you can add a home printer yourself if it does not require admin rights.
