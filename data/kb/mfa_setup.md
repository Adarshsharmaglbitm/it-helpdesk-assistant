# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

## Why MFA is required
Multi-factor authentication adds a second check, in addition to your password, every time you sign in to company systems from a new device or location. It is mandatory for all employees and contractors and protects your account even if your password is stolen.

## Enrolling in MFA
1. Install the company-approved authenticator app on your smartphone from the official app store.
2. On your laptop, go to `myaccount.company.internal` and choose **Security info > Add sign-in method**.
3. Select **Authenticator app** and scan the QR code shown on screen with the app.
4. Approve the test notification to finish enrollment.
5. Add your personal mobile number as a backup method so you can receive SMS codes.

New employees must complete enrollment within their first 3 days. Accounts that are not enrolled after 3 days are blocked from email until enrollment is done.

## Getting a new phone
Move MFA to your new phone before you wipe or hand in the old one:
1. Install the authenticator app on the new phone.
2. Sign in to `myaccount.company.internal` using the old phone to approve.
3. Add the new phone as a sign-in method and remove the old one.

If you already lost access to the old phone, sign in with the SMS backup code and register the new phone. If you have no working method at all, contact the IT Service Desk to reset your MFA registration after identity verification.

## Unexpected MFA prompts
If you receive an MFA approval request that you did not trigger, **deny it** and change your password immediately. Then report it as a security incident, because someone may know your password.
