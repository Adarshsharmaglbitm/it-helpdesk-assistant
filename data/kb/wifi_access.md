# Office Wi-Fi Access

## Available networks
- **Corp-Secure**: For company laptops only. Connects automatically using your device certificate; no password needed. Gives full access to internal systems.
- **Corp-Mobile**: For company-approved phones and tablets. Sign in with your company email and password.
- **Guest**: For visitors and personal devices. Internet access only, with no access to internal systems.

## Company laptop will not connect to Corp-Secure
1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on again.
2. Choose "Forget this network" for Corp-Secure, then reconnect.
3. Make sure the date and time on the laptop are correct, because the device certificate fails if the clock is wrong.
4. Restart the laptop.
If it still fails, connect with a network cable or the Guest network plus VPN and raise a ticket under **Network > Wi-Fi**. The device certificate may need to be renewed.

## Guest Wi-Fi for visitors
Visitors get Wi-Fi access through a voucher. The employee hosting the visitor requests the voucher at reception or through the visitor management system. Each voucher is valid for 1 day and up to 2 devices. Employees must not share their own credentials with visitors.

## Personal devices
Personal laptops and phones can only use the Guest network. Connecting personal devices to Corp-Secure or Corp-Mobile is not permitted.
