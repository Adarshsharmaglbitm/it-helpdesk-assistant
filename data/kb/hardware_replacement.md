# Hardware Replacement and Peripherals

## Hardware refresh program
Laptops are replaced automatically every 4 years. You will receive an email about 30 days before your laptop is due, with instructions to book a swap appointment. Your files in cloud storage are available on the new laptop immediately; local files outside synced folders must be backed up by you before the swap.

## Damaged laptop
If your laptop is physically damaged (cracked screen, liquid spill, broken keyboard):
1. Stop using it if there is liquid damage or a swollen battery, and unplug it.
2. Raise a ticket under **Hardware > Damaged Device** with photos of the damage.
3. IT provides a loaner laptop while yours is repaired. Repairs usually take 5 to 7 business days.
Accidental damage is covered. Repeated damage due to negligence may require manager approval for further repairs.

## Lost or stolen laptop
Report it to the IT Service Desk by phone within 1 hour so the device can be remotely locked and wiped. For theft, also file a police report and attach the report number to your ticket. A replacement is issued after manager approval, usually within 2 business days.

## Peripherals
Standard peripherals (mouse, keyboard, headset, laptop bag, one external monitor) can be requested through the self-service portal under **Hardware > Peripherals**. A second external monitor or an ergonomic keyboard needs manager approval. Items are collected from the IT desk or shipped to remote employees within 5 business days.

## Returning equipment
When you leave the company, return your laptop, badge, and all peripherals on your last working day. Remote employees receive a prepaid return shipping kit.
