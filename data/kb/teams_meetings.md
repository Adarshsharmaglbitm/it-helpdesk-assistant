# Microsoft Teams Audio, Video and Meetings

## Others cannot hear you
1. Check that you are not muted in the meeting and that the headset's own mute switch is off.
2. In Teams, go to **Settings > Devices** and select the correct microphone.
3. On Windows, check **Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone** and make sure Teams is allowed. On macOS, check **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone**.
4. Unplug and reconnect the headset, then rejoin the meeting.

## Camera is not working
- Close other apps that might be using the camera (such as other meeting apps).
- Check that the laptop's physical camera shutter, if it has one, is open.
- Confirm camera permission in the operating system privacy settings, as for the microphone.
- Select the right camera in **Settings > Devices**.

## Poor call quality
Choppy audio or frozen video usually means a weak network. Turn off your incoming video, close large downloads or uploads, and move closer to the Wi-Fi router. A wired connection gives the best quality. Approved headsets are available from the IT desk.

## Clearing the Teams cache
If Teams is slow, shows old data, or keeps crashing, clearing the cache often helps. Quit Teams fully, then sign out and sign back in. If the problem continues, uninstall and reinstall Teams from Software Center or Self Service.

## Meeting room equipment
Problems with conference room screens, cameras, or speakerphones should be reported to the Workplace AV team through the self-service portal under **Facilities > Meeting Rooms**, not as a laptop issue.
