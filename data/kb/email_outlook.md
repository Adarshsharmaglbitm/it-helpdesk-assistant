# Email and Outlook Issues

## Outlook is not receiving new emails
1. Check the status bar at the bottom of Outlook. If it says "Working offline", go to **Send/Receive > Work Offline** to turn offline mode off.
2. Confirm you have internet access and that webmail (`mail.company.internal`) shows the missing messages. If webmail has them, the problem is with the desktop app.
3. Close Outlook completely and reopen it.
4. If it still does not sync, repair the Outlook profile: **File > Account Settings > Account Settings > Repair**.

## Mailbox is full
Every mailbox has a 50 GB limit. At 49 GB you receive a warning, and at 50 GB you can no longer send email. To free up space:
- Empty the Deleted Items and Junk folders.
- Sort by size and delete or archive large attachments.
- Turn on the Online Archive, which gives an extra 100 GB for older mail. Request it through the self-service portal under **Email > Online Archive**.

## Access to a shared mailbox
Shared mailboxes (for example, team inboxes) are granted by the mailbox owner. Raise a request in the self-service portal under **Email > Shared Mailbox Access** and name the mailbox. The owner approves it, and the mailbox appears in Outlook automatically within 2 hours.

## Out-of-office replies
Set automatic replies from **File > Automatic Replies** in desktop Outlook, or **Settings > Mail > Automatic replies** in webmail. You can schedule start and end dates and set a different message for people outside the company.

## Suspicious emails
Do not click links or open attachments in unexpected emails. See the article on reporting phishing.
